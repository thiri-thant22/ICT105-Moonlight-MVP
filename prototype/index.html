Problem Statement

Many university students experience difficulty in managing their daily expenses and saving money. They often forget where their money goes because expenses are not recorded consistently. This makes it difficult to control spending and plan future budgets.

Our Solution

Budget Tracker provides an easy way to record expenses, organize spending by category, search transaction history, and monitor remaining budget through a clean dashboard. It helps users develop better financial habits and improve personal money management.
