# Sales Scenario

## 1. Project Title
Budget Tracker Website

## 2. Target User / Customer
- Primary user: Students and young professionals
- Secondary user: Anyone who wants to manage personal finances
- Decision-maker or admin role, if any: None

## 3. Situation / Context
A university student receives monthly income from parents or part-time work but often spends money without recording expenses. At the end of the month, they cannot remember where their money went and find it difficult to manage their budget.

## 4. Pain Point
Users forget to record daily expenses, have difficulty tracking spending habits, and often exceed their planned budget because they do not have a simple financial management tool.

## 5. Product Introduction
The Budget Tracker Website is introduced through the project landing page and a live prototype demo. Users can quickly see how the system records income and expenses, tracks transactions, and displays financial summaries.

## 6. Value Message
For students and young professionals who struggle to manage daily expenses, our Budget Tracker Website helps them control their finances by recording income and expenses, tracking transactions, and providing a real-time dashboard summary.

## 7. Sales Conversation / Pitch Moment
"Managing your money doesn't have to be difficult. Our Budget Tracker Website lets you record income and expenses in seconds, search your transaction history, and instantly view your remaining balance through a simple dashboard. It helps you understand your spending habits and make better financial decisions."

## 8. Expected User Action
- Try the demo
- Add income and expense records
- View the dashboard summary
- Search transaction records
- Give feedback

## 9. Related Requirements
- FR-03 – Add Expense Form
- FR-06 – Search and Filter
- FR-08 – Record Management
- FR-12 – Dashboard Summary
