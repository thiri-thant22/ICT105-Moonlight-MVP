# Landing Page Content

## Project Title
Budget Tracker Website

## Target User
Students and young professionals who want to manage their income and expenses more effectively.

## Headline
Track your money. Control your future.

## Subheadline
Budget Tracker helps users record income and expenses, monitor spending habits, and view financial summaries in one place. It makes personal financial management simple and convenient.

## Problem Statement
Many students and young professionals do not keep track of their daily expenses. They often rely on memory, overspend their budget, and find it difficult to understand where their money goes.

## Solution Description
The Budget Tracker Website allows users to record income and expenses, organize transactions by category, search records easily, and monitor their financial status through a real-time dashboard.

## Key Features

| Feature | Requirement ID | User Value | Prototype Screen/Module |
|---------|----------------|------------|--------------------------|
| Add Expense | FR-03 | Record daily expenses quickly | Add Expense Page |
| Search & Filter | FR-06 | Find transactions easily | Search Page |
| Record Management | FR-08 | Edit and manage financial records | Expense List |
| Dashboard Summary | FR-12 | View income, expenses, and remaining balance | Dashboard |

## Benefits
- Easily monitor daily spending.
- Better control of personal finances.
- Save time by organizing financial records automatically.
- Make better budgeting decisions.
- Understand spending habits through dashboard summaries.

## Call-to-Action
- Try the Demo
- Add Your First Expense
- View Dashboard
- Start Tracking Your Budget
- Give Feedback

## Responsible Data Message
Budget Tracker only collects information required to manage personal finances, such as income, expenses, categories, dates, and optional notes. No sensitive personal information, such as bank account numbers or credit card details, is collected. User data is used only for demonstration and prototype testing purposes.
