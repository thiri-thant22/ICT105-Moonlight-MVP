# Digital Go-to-Market Plan

## Product / Prototype Name

Budget Tracker Website

## Target Early Users

University students and young professionals who want to manage their income and expenses more effectively.

## Main Value Proposition

A simple and easy-to-use website that helps users record income and expenses, track spending habits, and manage their budget efficiently.

## Channel Plan

| Channel | Target User | Message Angle | User Action Expected | Metric |
|---------|-------------|---------------|----------------------|--------|
| Facebook Group | University students | Easily manage your daily expenses with Budget Tracker | Visit the website and test the prototype | Landing page visits |
| LINE Group | Students and friends | Record your expenses in just a few clicks | Try the Add Expense feature | Demo attempts |
| Instagram Story | Young adults | Take control of your spending and save more money | Open the website and give feedback | Feedback responses |
| Class Presentation | Lecturers and classmates | Demonstrate how Budget Tracker helps manage personal finances | Test the prototype and complete tasks | Task completion rate |

## Launch Sequence

1. Prepare the Budget Tracker website and landing page.
2. Share the prototype link through Facebook, LINE, and Instagram.
3. Invite classmates and friends to test the website.
4. Collect user feedback using Google Forms.
5. Review user comments and improve the prototype.
6. Update the system based on testing results.

## Risk and Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Low number of users testing the prototype | Share the website through multiple social media channels and invite more classmates. |
| Users do not understand how to use the website | Add clear instructions and simplify the interface. |
| Users find bugs during testing | Fix reported issues quickly and release an updated version. |
| Low feedback response rate | Encourage users to complete the feedback form after testing. |
